ACADEMIC HORIZON

Research Vision & Strategic Directions

Bridging high-dimensional clinical evidence synthesis with precision oncology and quantitative decision science.

🎯 Precision Immuno-Oncology

Deconstructing differential survival benefits and toxicity trade-offs in immune checkpoint inhibitor combinations (anti-PD-1/PD-L1 + anti-CTLA-4) stratified by molecular and histopathologic biomarkers (PD-L1 TPS/CPS expression, tumor mutational burden, and genomic driver alterations).

⚖️ Advanced Sparse Synthesis

Formulating robust penalization algorithms (Firth logistic regression) and exact beta-binomial frameworks for zero-event treatment arms, coupled with Copas non-ignorable selection modeling to account for reporting and publication biases in competitive trial landscapes.

🩺 Health Economics & Value of Information

Integrating network meta-analytic effect vectors into decision-analytic Markov state-transition models, minimal clinically important difference (MCID) frontiers, and Expected Value of Perfect Information (EVPI) distributions to guide evidence-based health technology assessment.
